# LuxeResort Manager: Marketing Strategy & Content Calendar
**"Scaling Your Paradise through Automation and Authority"**

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The LuxeResort Manager marketing strategy is built around the "Owner-Operator Freedom" narrative. We don't just sell software; we sell a lifestyle of automated growth, operational peace of mind, and elite guest experiences.

## 2. Target Audience (The Elite Operators)
1.  **The Overwhelmed Owner:** Manages 5–20 units using spreadsheets and WhatsApp. Stressed by double bookings and manual tasks.
2.  **The Digital Modernizer:** Already using basic plugins but wants a "5-star" look and more professional automation (iCal sync, staff roles).
3.  **The Agency Partner:** Developers or marketers who build sites for resorts and need a reliable, all-in-one engine for their clients.

## 3. Core Messaging Pillars
*   **Automation = Freedom:** Reclaim 10+ hours a week. Let the system handle the midnight inquiries.
*   **Frictionless Conversion:** 5-step booking flow designed to turn visitors into confirmed guests on any device.
*   **Operational Synergy:** Front-of-house and back-of-house working in perfect harmony.
*   **Smart Growth:** Revenue intelligence and loyalty programs that turn one-time stays into lifetime value.

## 5. Key Marketing Channels
*   **Content Marketing:** Weekly blog posts on "Resort Operational Efficiency" and "Guest Retention Strategies."
*   **Paid Social:** Meta ads targeting "Property Management," "Resort Owners," and "Hospitality Professionals" using video demos.
*   **Affiliate/Agency Program:** Partner with WordPress agencies to bundle LuxeResort into their boutique hotel packages.
*   **Email Automation:** Drip campaign for abandoned leads captured via the `[resort_lead_form]`.

## 6. Conversion Optimization
*   **Lead Capture:** Use the Gated Content feature to offer a free "Resort Efficiency Audit" or "Elite Guest Checklist."
*   **Social Proof:** Highlight user success stories and "Zero Double Booking" testimonials prominently.
