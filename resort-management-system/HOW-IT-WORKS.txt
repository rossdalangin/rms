LuxeResort Manager is a complete "Paradise-in-a-Box" solution for WordPress. This guide explains how the system operates and where to find the essential documentation for each stakeholder.

2. FOR THE GUEST (The Frictionless Journey)
-------------------------------------------
The guest experience is a smooth, 5-step AJAX process designed to maximize conversions:
Step 1: Search - Real-time availability across all villas.
Step 2: Selection - "Tropical Modern" grid showing rooms with clear pricing.
Step 3: Extras - Upselling of luxury services (Spa, Tours, Transfers).
Step 4: Guest Info - Simple collection of preferences and automatic account creation.
Step 5: Payment - Secure checkout (Stripe, PayPal, WooCommerce, or Offline).
Step 6: Management - Private dashboard for service requests, self check-in, and loyalty points.

---

3. FOR THE ADMIN (The Operational Hub)
--------------------------------------
The "LuxeResort" menu in WordPress is the central command center:
* Executive Dashboard: Real-time revenue, occupancy, and guest summary widgets.
* Reservation Calendar: Interactive timeline with 2-way iCal synchronization (Airbnb/Booking.com).
* Housekeeping Dashboard: Real-time room status tracking for operational staff.
* Smart Pricing: Market Intelligence to track competitors and occupancy-based rate optimization.
* CRM & Marketing: Deep integration with HubSpot, Zoho, and Mailchimp.
* Loyalty Program: Automatic point calculation and redemption for repeat guests.

---

4. SYSTEM AUTOMATIONS (Under the Hood)
--------------------------------------
* Hourly Inventory Cleanup: Releases rooms held by abandoned "pending" bookings.
* Automated Concierge: Sends pre-arrival and post-departure emails via cron jobs.
* Dynamic Currency Conversion: Instant switching between PHP, USD, EUR, and GBP.
* Multi-Role Access: Specific capabilities for 'Resort Staff' to protect sensitive financial data.

LuxeResort Manager: Helping you spend less time on software, and more time on hospitality.
